A reminder, not a diagnosis

You don't have to
figure it all out alone.

Feeling pressure doesn't mean you're failing. You deserve care, understanding and support far beyond grades.

↗

Make the next step smaller

Instead of solving the whole week in your head, choose one manageable thing for the next ten minutes. A little progress is still progress.

◯

Talk to a real person

If the pressure keeps feeling heavy, consider speaking with someone you trust—a friend, family member, teacher, or school counsellor. You don't have to find the perfect words first.

✳

Rest is part of being human

Try a glass of water, a short walk, music, or a few minutes away from your screen. Pick what feels kind and possible for you right now.

⌁

Your worth isn't a number

Marks can matter for particular opportunities, but they cannot measure your whole character, your kindness, your curiosity, or your future.

UNGRADED offers restorative pauses and supportive ideas; it is not a medical or mental-health diagnostic service. If you are struggling, reaching out to a trusted adult or qualified health professional can make all the difference.
